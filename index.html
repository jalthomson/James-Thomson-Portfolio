Hybrid rocket nozzle: design, analysis, test fire

I sized and modeled an 80% bell nozzle for a hybrid thruster optimized for operation at 45,000 ft. The design went through three design reviews, then I 3D-printed it in ABS resin and fired it on the group's test stand.
